Statements

Intended Use

BAM-F017 dissolved in absolute ethanol is certified for use as a fluorescence quantum yield standard for the relative determination of fluorescence quantum yields of transparent luminophore solutions.

Storage Information

This certificate is valid for 2 years after dispatch, if the solid CRM is stored at 2 - 8 °C in the dark. BAM-F017 can be shipped at room temperature.

Handling Instructions

Please follow the Standard Operation Procedure (SOP) provided with the material.

Metrological Traceability

The certified fluorescence quantum yield value was determined with a calibrated custom-built integrating sphere setup.
